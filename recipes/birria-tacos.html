Birria Tacos

Description

Birria tacos are crispy corn tortillas filled with slow-cooked, tender beef that has been braised in a rich blend of dried chilies, spices, and herbs. They're typically served with a flavorful consommé for dipping, creating a savory, juicy, and slightly spicy dish that has become a favorite in Mexican cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck or brisket
  • 8 to 10 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, diced
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella or Oaxaca cheese
  • 3 dried guajillo chilies
  • 2 dried ancho chilies
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooking oil

Method:

  1. Toast and soak the dried chilies until softened, then blend them with the garlic, cumin, oregano, and a little beef stock to make a smooth sauce.
  2. Coat the beef with the sauce and simmer with the remaining beef stock for 3–4 hours, or until the meat is fall-apart tender. Shred the beef.
  3. Dip each tortilla into the cooking broth (consommé) and place it in a hot pan.
  4. Add cheese, shredded birria beef, onion, and cilantro, then fold the tortilla in half.
  5. Cook until both sides are crispy and the cheese has melted.
  6. Serve with a bowl of the consommé for dipping and garnish with extra onion, cilantro, and lime wedges.
